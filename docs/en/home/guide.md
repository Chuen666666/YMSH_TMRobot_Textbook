# Site Guide

Whenever this site says "robot arm", it means the two TM5S robot arms in the YMSH library.

::: info Recommendation
For a better reading experience, use a tablet or computer. Students in the library can also borrow the library computers.
:::

::: danger Warning
This textbook is only for **Taipei Municipal Yang Ming Senior High School students** for learning purposes.

The contents of this textbook **must not be shared outside the school in any form**.

Do not give the textbook contents or the shared password to **non-YMSH students** or any unauthorized third party.
:::

## Lesson Levels

The textbook is split into three levels:

- Basic: for complete beginners who want to get hands-on with the robot arm fast
- Intermediate: for newer competitors who want the must-have competition skills
- Advanced: experience and techniques for players who want strong competition results

Pick the level that fits your current skill and start from there.

## Booking Practice

The robot arms are currently managed by the library. Please ask the library director before practicing.

When using the robot arm, please be decent: put the arm and all parts back after use, and restore the environment to its original state.

If you find any damage, or if you want to buy extra end tools, tell the library director.
