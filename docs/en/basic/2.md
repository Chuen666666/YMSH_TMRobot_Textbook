## Button Functions

There are many buttons near the robot arm's end module. Some competitions may lock certain buttons, so here we only mention the two buttons used most often. For the other buttons, such as `SET` and `VISION`, go ahead and try pressing them yourself to see what they do and whether they are available.

![Buttons on the robot arm](/button_on_eye.jpg)

- `POINT` button: after pressing it, you can set a `Point` in the project. Later chapters explain this in detail.
- `FREE` button: only while holding `FREE` can you directly pull the robot arm by hand.
