# 3. Storage Checklist

## Transparent Box

- Put Landmark blocks back into the bag
- Put mahjong blocks back into the bag
- Put the small board for mahjong blocks back into the bag
- Stack the calibration board on the top layer
- Make sure the lid is closed

## Cables and External Devices

- Put the box back in its original place
- Push the large display back to its original place
- Unplug the HDMI cable and put it back
- Unplug the power cable, roll it up, and put it back inside the robot arm shell
- Put the controller back where it belongs, either on the metal controller holder or another metal area

## Robot Arm (Standard Pose)

1. Click `≡` in the top-left corner, then click `Settings` &rarr; `Pose Settings`, and choose `Standard Pose`
2. Hold `PLAY` on the controller until the adjustment is complete
3. Click `≡` again, then click `Shutdown`; after the warning window appears, click `OK` to shut down normally
