# 1. Before and After Practice

## Booking Practice

For self practice, ask the library director first to book the robot arm.

## During Practice

If any equipment is damaged, actively tell the library or Teacher Chia-Ching.

## After Practice

Put everything back in its assigned place. For storage details, see [this article](/en/basic/3).

## Items

|Item|Start of use<br>(connect)|End of use<br>(unplug and store)|
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|Cable on the right side of the robot arm metal case|The cable is inside the metal case. Connect it to the extension cord.|Hide it back inside the metal case.|
|Cable under the robot arm metal case|Connect it to the extension cord.|Hide it back underneath.|
|Extension cord|Make sure it is switched on and connected to the floor outlet.|Cabinet near the library entrance.|
|HDMI cable|There is more than one. It may be in the case or near the large display. Connect the large display (IN) and the robot control box (OUT).|Roll it up and put it back.|
