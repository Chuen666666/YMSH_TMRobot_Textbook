# 5. Self Practice

## Task

Use visual recognition to recognize a QR Code, English text, or numbers, then display the recognition result.

## Verification

Place an object with clear text, or a complete and clear QR Code, under the robot arm. Check whether the screen correctly displays the information.

## Answer

1. `Point` (Robot Base): first move to a suitable position so the later vision recognition task can see the workpiece.
2. `Vision` (Robot Base, Inspection): use it to recognize a QR Code, English text, or numbers.
3. `Display`: show the scanned result (variable) in `Display`.

## Other Tasks

Practice the self-practice chapters in [Basic](/en/basic/6) and [Intermediate](/en/intermediate/4) until you are comfortable, then make your own variations for self practice.

You can even play with unfamiliar nodes or buttons after you get tired of the standard practice. A lot of functions were discovered by us just messing around, so I also recommend that you try things yourself and find ways to make all kinds of wild ideas real.
